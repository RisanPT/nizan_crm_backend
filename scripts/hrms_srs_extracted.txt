4.4 Data integrity rules
DR-1 — One EmploymentGrade per employee per review period, enforced by a unique compound index.
DR-2 — One LeaveBalance per employee, leave type and year, enforced by a unique compound index.
DR-3 — The existing unique index on Salary by employee, month and year is retained.
DR-4 — A paid salary record is immutable. Corrections are made by cancelling and reissuing, never by editing in place.
DR-5 — All schema changes are additive with defaults, so existing production documents remain valid without migration
(constraint C-4).
